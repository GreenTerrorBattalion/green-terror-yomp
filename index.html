What a Yomp is

Yomp stands for “Your Own March Pace.” It is a long-distance hike at your own speed, with the goal of finishing the chosen stage within 24 hours. The route runs through the capital, Arlington and along the C&O Canal, with stops at embassies along the way.

25 mi 36 mi 50 mi

Silver adds 11 miles to Bronze. Gold adds 14 more.

Where your gift goes

The Allied Forces Foundation brings together wounded, ill, and injured service people and veterans, and their caregivers, from the U.S., the U.K. and allied nations. Peer-led outdoor events build teamwork and a renewed sense of purpose. The Warrior Yomp raises funds and awareness for that mission.

Who we are

The Green Terror Battalion is an ROTC program based in Maryland, hosted at:

  • McDaniel College
  • Mount St. Mary’s University
  • Hood College

Walk with us in spirit. A gift of any size moves our team toward its goal and supports veterans who are still healing.
